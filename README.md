## 01. ABOUT ALIPAX

<img align="right" width="150" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/ALIPAX-DIGITAL%20TECHNOLOGY-173B2A?style=for-the-badge" alt="Alipax"/>

**ALIPAX** is a technology brand focused on software engineering, digital solutions, and modern web experiences.

We explore the intersection of technology, design, and engineering to transform ideas into meaningful digital products.

Our philosophy is simple: technology should solve problems, simplify experiences, and create lasting value.

We approach every project with curiosity, precision, and a commitment to continuous improvement.
